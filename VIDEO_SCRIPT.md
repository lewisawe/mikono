# Mikono — Video Script (90-100s cut)

Screen recording with voiceover. Keep it under 100 seconds; judges watch many.

---

**[0:00-0:10] Hook — screen: match board**
VO: "Most giving apps move money. Mikono moves hands — skills and time. And it
matches them with Snowflake Cortex."

**[0:10-0:30] The proof — screen: zoom the Uhuru ↔ Amina card**
VO: "This school wrote 'our lab machines won't switch on.' This volunteer wrote
'I fix broken laptops.' Zero shared words — look, the keyword bar is empty. But
Cortex reads meaning, so it pairs them. That's the idea in one card."

**[0:30-0:55] Live — screen: 'I need help' tab, paste and submit**
VO: "It's not hardcoded. I'll type a brand-new need..."
(paste the clinic-computers line, click, show spinner then result)
VO: "...Cortex classifies it as tech repair and finds the match live, in the
warehouse."

**[0:55-1:10] Honesty — screen: paste a no-fit offer**
VO: "And when nothing fits, it says so, instead of faking a match."

**[1:10-1:20] Bilingual — screen: paste a Swahili offer, submit**
VO: "It even works across languages — here's an offer in Swahili, matched to the
same cause. Cortex translates, then matches."

**[1:20-1:35] Tech — screen: the SQL file 04_match.sql**
VO: "Five Cortex functions do it all in SQL: embeddings, cosine ranking,
classification, translation, and a generated next step on every match. Nothing
leaves Snowflake."

**[1:35-1:40] Close — screen: title**
VO: "Mikono. Swahili for hands."

---

## Recording tips
- 1280x720 or 1080p, hide bookmarks bar.
- Pre-type the paste examples in a notes app to avoid typos on camera.
- Let the spinner show; it proves live compute.
- Keep cursor movements slow and deliberate.
